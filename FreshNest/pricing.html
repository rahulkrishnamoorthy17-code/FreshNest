Pricing questions, answered

Will every stain disappear?

No provider can promise complete removal of every stain. Results depend on substance, age, previous products and fiber condition; we explain risks before treatment.

How long will the surface take to dry?

Most suitable carpets and sofas dry in about 4-6 hours with airflow. Heavy upholstery, humid conditions or thick underlay can take longer, so we provide material-specific guidance.

Are the solutions safe around children and pets?

We select family-conscious products and rinse residues where appropriate. Children and pets should avoid treated surfaces until they are completely dry.

Do I need to move furniture?

Please clear small items and valuables before arrival. Our technician can discuss light furniture access, but heavy wardrobes and electronics must stay in place.

What does the 7-day re-clean guarantee cover?

Report an eligible cleaning-quality concern within seven days and we will assess a complimentary targeted re-clean. Permanent stains, pre-existing damage and new spills are excluded.

Can you clean velvet or silk?

Certain fibers require specialist care and may not tolerate extraction. We inspect labels and test suitable areas, recommending an alternative if wet cleaning is unsafe.

Are weekend visits available?

Saturday visits are offered subject to available technician slots. Time availability in this demo is simulated and does not reserve a real technician.

When is payment due?

The demo dashboard records sample invoices and payments only. In a real service, payment terms are confirmed during booking and are never charged through this website.
