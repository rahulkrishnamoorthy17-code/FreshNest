What the service includes

Our technicians inspect the care label, check for existing marks and choose a method matched to your upholstery. We avoid forcing hot water extraction onto fragile or unsuitable coverings.

01

Inspect and test

We confirm the fabric type, note discoloration and test an inconspicuous patch before applying products to visible areas.

02

Pre-vacuum and pre-treat

Loose grit and crumbs are removed, then appropriate stain pre-treatment is applied to traffic areas and marks.

03

Controlled cleaning

Where suitable, measured hot water extraction rinses the surface and recovers moisture. Sensitive materials get a gentler method or referral.

04

Inspect and dry

We look for remaining marks, give after-care tips and recommend air circulation while fabrics dry fully.

Plan for success

How to prepare and care for your space

A few simple steps before and after cleaning help our team work carefully and give your freshly treated furnishings enough drying time.

Before we arrive

Remove small items and fragile decor from the cleaning area. Point out old stains, previous DIY products and damaged seams. Keep children and pets away from the work zone while equipment is operating.

During cleaning

Our technician explains any colorfastness or shrinkage risks. Water temperature and moisture are adjusted to the fabric. We will not use extraction on materials that fail the pre-test.

After cleaning

Provide airflow with fans or windows if weather permits. Do not replace cushions, covers or bedding until completely dry. Avoid heavy use until the technician's drying guidance has been met.
