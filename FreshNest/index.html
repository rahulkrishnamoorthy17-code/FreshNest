The FreshNest difference

Why thoughtful cleaning matters

Good cleaning means much more than a machine and a quick pass. Our approach balances stain care, fabric knowledge, transparent estimates and responsible aftercare.

01

Family-conscious products

Our team chooses low-residue solutions and uses the correct dilution for each surface. Children and pets should stay off the cleaned area until it has dried.

02

Fabric-aware inspection

We check fiber type, colorfastness, existing wear and care labels before treatment. Delicate materials are flagged rather than subjected to aggressive extraction.

03

Upfront estimates

You see a clear starting estimate before requesting your visit. A technician confirms room dimensions and any stain-specific add-ons before work starts.

04

Targeted stain care

Coffee, food, mud and pet stains each need different pre-treatment. We explain likely results in advance because some permanent dye changes cannot be reversed.

05

Practical drying advice

Many carpets dry in around four to six hours with airflow and normal weather. Thick upholstery, humid days and low ventilation can take longer.

06

Seven-day re-clean promise

If an eligible cleaned area still needs attention, contact us within seven days of service. We review the concern and arrange a targeted re-clean when covered by our policy.

Before your first cleaning, know what to expect

We want every visit to be comfortable and predictable. These answers cover safety, drying, stains, appointments and recurring cleaning without overstating what a treatment can achieve.

Ask our team

Most standard carpet jobs are expected to dry in about four to six hours with good ventilation. Heavy pile, humidity or unusually soiled areas can need longer; your technician will share a practical estimate after cleaning.

We select low-residue products with household use in mind and follow correct dilution and rinsing practices. Keep children and pets off damp fabric until the area is fully dry and follow any product-specific advice.

Some fresh food and drink marks respond very well, but no responsible cleaner can guarantee every stain. Dye transfer, bleach damage and older set-in stains may remain even after a careful treatment.

Please clear small belongings, toys and breakable objects before the team arrives. We can discuss light furniture movement at booking, but large or fragile items may need to remain in place.

Our sample service coverage includes Peelamedu, RS Puram, Gandhipuram, Singanallur and Saravanampatti. Enter your location when booking so the team can confirm whether a slot is available.

We typically inspect and vacuum, pre-treat marks, agitate where appropriate and perform controlled hot water extraction. The final pass removes as much moisture as practical and includes care instructions.

Contact the team within seven days if an eligible area does not meet the agreed cleaning standard. We assess the concern and, if covered, schedule a targeted follow-up clean; permanent stains and new spills are excluded.
